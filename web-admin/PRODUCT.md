# FreshMart

Vietnamese retail operations app for administrators, store managers and staff.
The user describes its role as similar to KiotViet, with warehouse monitoring and IoT.

## Primary tasks
- Sell available stock, prepare an invoice and complete its actual payment flow.
- Receive supplier goods, track purchase orders, transfer and count inventory.
- Manage products, customers, suppliers, staff and branch access.
- Review operational reports, forecasting and warehouse events.

## Design brief
The owner explicitly requested a substantial interface improvement, including internal
forms and pages, using Taste Skill and Impeccable. Prior iterations looked crude and
overly generated. Prioritize readable Vietnamese, familiar task structure, cohesive
tables/forms and clear actions over decorative dashboard cards. Desktop is the primary
work surface; mobile navigation and forms must remain usable.

## Constraints
Preserve permissions, business calculations, API contracts and real payment/inventory
transitions. Do not invent live states, statistics or successful transactions for styling.
Use explicitly mocked data only in screenshot tests. Existing logo and product images
remain the product's visual assets.
